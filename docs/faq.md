__Does it change my existing dashboards?__

No. Until you choose a segment or time range for a widget or set a dashboard default, everything behaves exactly like standard Matomo.

__Which segments can I use?__

Every saved Matomo segment you are allowed to see for the website. Open the segment menu in the widget header; a search field filters long lists. To create a new segment, choose "New segment" in the menu: it opens Matomo's segment editor, and the saved segment is then available in the menu.

__Which time ranges can a widget have?__

Today, yesterday, the last 7 days, the last 30 days, this week, this month and this year. The widget keeps its time range when you change the date of the dashboard. "×" in the bar under the widget title brings it back to the dashboard's date.

__Which widgets support a segment or time range of their own?__

Report widgets that show a table, for example Channel Types, Pages, Entry pages, Countries, Device type or Campaigns. Graphs and charts, sparklines, the overview widgets (Visits Overview, Goals and Ecommerce overview), the Real-time widgets and the Visitor Map load their data themselves in the browser. They show no segment or time range buttons and follow the segment of the whole dashboard.

__How do I filter graphs and the Real-time widget then?__

With the dashboard default segment above the widgets. It filters the whole dashboard, including graphs, sparklines, the Real-time widgets and the Visitor Map. When you leave the dashboard, the segment you had before comes back.

__Can the same widget appear twice with different segments?__

Yes. Add the widget twice and give each copy its own segment, for example to compare all visits with mobile visitors side by side.

__Are my choices the same on every website?__

No. Segments and time ranges are stored per user, dashboard and website, so each website keeps its own setup.

__What do anonymous visitors of a public dashboard see?__

The dashboard with its normal segment. Segment choices belong to signed-in users, so the plugin stays inactive for anonymous visitors.

__Does it work with Feinwerk Dashboard Sharing?__

Yes. When you share a dashboard, the copies of your colleagues take the segments and time ranges of your widgets along.
